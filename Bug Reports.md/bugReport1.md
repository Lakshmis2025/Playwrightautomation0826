Bug ID: BUF-001
Title: Social share buttons do not respond when clicked.
Environment:
Browser: Google Chrome
Operating System: Windows 10
Website: http://academybugs.com/

Preconditions:
1. The AcademyBugs website is open.
2. The user is on the "Examples of Bugs" page.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Open http://academybugs.com/
2. Find "Social share buttons do not work."
3. Click the arrow button on the card.
4. Click a social media share button.

Expected Result: 
The selected social media website should open and allow the user to share the content.

Actual Result:
Nothing happens when the social media share button is clicked.

Severity:
Medium

Priority:
Medium

Evidence:
Screenshot or screen recordings attached.

Status: New
