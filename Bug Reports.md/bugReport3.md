Bug ID: BUG-005
Title: "Video player doesn’t work"
Environment:
Browser: Google Chrome
Operating System: Windows 10
Website: http://academybugs.com/

Preconditions:
1. The AcademyBugs website is open.
2. The user is on the "Examples of Bugs" page.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Open http://academybugs.com/
2. Find Video player doesn’t work.
3. Click the arrow button on the card.
4. Try to play the videos on the left column.

Expected Result: 
Video players should quickly buffer and play video files.

A black screen is shown instead of videos in the left column
